# Meridian Trust Bank (an invented bank, for demonstration only)
# Product terms MTB-PRD-052: Saver Account, terms

Version 1.5. Owner: Head of Retail Banking. Approved by the Product Committee, 3 March 2026. Next review: March 2027.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 These terms apply to the Saver Account of an individual customer. All amounts are in US dollars.

## 2. Interest

2.1 Interest is worked out each day on the closing balance and paid on the last day of each month.

2.2 Each part of the balance earns the rate of its tier. The first USD 5,000.00 earns 1.50 percent a year. The part above USD 5,000.00 and up to USD 25,000.00 earns 2.25 percent a year. The part above USD 25,000.00 earns 2.75 percent a year.

2.3 The bank gives 30 days' written notice before it lowers a rate.

## 3. Paying in and withdrawing

3.1 A customer may pay into the account at any time, by transfer from the customer's own accounts or from other people.

3.2 A withdrawal needs 14 days' notice, given to the bank in writing or through online banking.

3.3 A customer who asks to withdraw without giving notice may do so in a branch. The bank then takes 14 days' interest on the amount withdrawn.

## 4. Fees

4.1 There is no monthly fee on the Saver Account.

## 5. Closing the account

5.1 A customer may close the account after the notice in clause 3.2 has been given. Interest earned to the day of closing is paid with the balance.
