# Meridian Trust Bank (an invented bank, for demonstration only)
# Product terms MTB-PRD-051: Everyday Current Account, terms and fees

Version 2.0. Owner: Head of Retail Banking. Approved by the Product Committee, 3 March 2026. Next review: March 2027.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 These terms apply to the Everyday Current Account of an individual customer. All amounts are in US dollars.

## 2. Monthly account fee

2.1 The monthly account fee is USD 12.00. It is charged once in each calendar month.

2.2 The fee is waived for a calendar month if the total paid into the account in the previous calendar month was at least USD 3,000.00. Payments in include salary and transfers from other people. Transfers from the customer's own accounts with the bank do not count.

2.3 Only one monthly account fee is charged in a calendar month. If a second one is charged, the extra amount is refunded once the bank has confirmed it.

2.4 The bank gives 60 days' written notice before it raises the monthly account fee.

## 3. Overdrafts

3.1 An account may be overdrawn only up to a limit the bank has agreed with the customer in writing.

3.2 When an account is overdrawn without an agreed limit, or beyond it, the bank charges an overdraft fee of USD 20.00. It is charged once in a calendar month, however many days the account is overdrawn.

3.3 The bank may refuse a payment that would overdraw the account.

## 4. Cards

4.1 A replacement debit card costs USD 9.00. There is no charge when the card has expired or is faulty.

## 5. Payments

5.1 Transfers to another account in Meridia, in US dollars, are free of charge.

5.2 The fee for a payment sent to an account outside Meridia is set in the Tariff of Charges.

## 6. Changes and complaints

6.1 A customer who disagrees with a charge may complain to the bank. The complaint is handled under the bank's complaints procedure.
