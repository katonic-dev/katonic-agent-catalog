# Help Articles (invented)

Rowanmere Group is invented, and so is this document. It is not the document of any real retailer.

The help articles of Rowanmere Market's online shop, as the customer care team uses them. Twelve articles, HA-01 to HA-12.

## HA-01. Delivery times and charges

Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days and costs USD 4.95. It is free when the goods in the order come to USD 60.00 or more. Next-day delivery costs USD 7.95 and is offered on orders placed before 14:00.

## HA-02. Click and collect

An order placed before 15:00 is ready at the store within 4 hours. We send a message when it is ready. The store holds it for 5 days. Bring the order number when you collect. Click and collect is free.

## HA-03. Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order in your account for 60 minutes after you place it, or until its status becomes packed, whichever comes first. After that the order cannot be changed, and the item can be returned once it arrives.

## HA-04. How to return an item

Ask for a return in your account, under the order, within 30 days of delivery. We check the request against the Returns Policy and send a prepaid label when it is approved. An item can also be handed in at any Rowanmere Market store with the order number.

## HA-05. When a refund arrives

A refund goes to the payment method used for the order, within 5 working days of the day the return is approved. Your bank may take a further 2 days to show it.

## HA-06. Ways to pay

The online shop takes debit cards, credit cards and Rowanmere gift cards. It does not take cash or payment on collection: a click and collect order is paid for when it is placed.

## HA-07. Gift cards

A Rowanmere gift card is valid for 24 months from the day it was bought. It can be used together with a payment card in one order. The balance is shown in your account under Gift cards. A gift card cannot be exchanged for cash.

## HA-08. Rowanmere Rewards points

You earn 1 point for each dollar spent. Points show in your account within 48 hours of delivery or collection. Points lapse when 12 months pass without a purchase.

## HA-09. A missing or damaged item in a delivery

Tell us within 7 days of delivery. Send the order number, the name of the item and, for a damaged item, a photograph. A member of the care team then looks at the order and writes back.

## HA-10. Substitutions in a grocery order

When an item is out of stock we may send the nearest one in its place, at no higher price. You can hand a substitute back to the driver and it is refunded within 5 working days. You can switch substitutions off in your account under Shopping preferences.

## HA-11. Your account: password and email address

To reset a password, choose Forgotten password on the sign-in page and follow the link we send. To change the email address on an account, sign in, open Account details and choose Change email. We send a confirmation to both the old and the new address.

## HA-12. Delivery to a neighbour or a safe place

When nobody is in, the carrier may leave a parcel with a neighbour and puts a card through your door saying where it is. You can switch this off in your account under Delivery preferences. Chilled and frozen food is never left with a neighbour.
