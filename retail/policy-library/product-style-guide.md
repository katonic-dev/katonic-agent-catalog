# Product Style Guide (invented)

Rowanmere Group is invented, and so is this document. It is not the document of any real retailer.

Version: 1.3. Owner: Head of E-commerce. It covers the descriptions and image tags of Rowanmere Market's online shop.

## PS-1. Length

A description is 40 to 80 words, in two to four plain sentences.

## PS-2. How it opens

The first sentence says what the product is. No slogan and no question.

## PS-3. Words that are not used

These words are not used in a description: best, finest, perfect, amazing, delicious, healthy, superfood, guilt-free, miracle, world-class.

## PS-4. Claims

A description makes no health or medical claim. It makes no claim the supplier's specification does not make: the claims field of the specification is the only source for words such as free-range, organic or vegan.

## PS-5. Net quantity and pack size

The net quantity and the pack size are written exactly as the supplier's specification gives them.

## PS-6. Allergens

The allergen statement is copied word for word from the supplier's specification into its own field. It is never worked out from the ingredients, from the name or from a picture.

## PS-7. Country of origin

The country of origin is written as the supplier's specification gives it.

## PS-8. Image tags

Each product has three to six image tags, in lower case. A tag names something the image description says is in the picture, and nothing else.

## PS-9. Age-restricted lines

The description of an age-restricted line ends with the sentence: For adults aged 18 or over.

## PS-10. When a picture and the specification disagree

The specification is used. The difference is listed for the content lead, who asks the supplier.

## What this guide does not decide

This guide does not publish a listing. A content lead reads every description and publishes it. The guide says nothing about a price or a promotion.
