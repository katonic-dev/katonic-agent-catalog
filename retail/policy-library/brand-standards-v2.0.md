# Rowanmere Group (an invented company, for demonstration only)
# Brand Standards

**SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION. Every standard, number and name here is invented. It is not the operating standard of any real brand.**

- Document: Brand Standards
- Version: 2.0
- Effective date: 1 August 2026
- Owner: Head of Store Operations
- Applies to: every Rowanmere Market store and every Rowanmere Kitchen restaurant, company-run or franchised

## How a visit is scored

An area manager scores each item of the visit checklist against one standard: 2 when the standard is met, 1 when it is partly met, 0 when it is not met. An item scored 1 or 0 is a finding. Something the area manager writes in the notes is a finding too, when it can be tied to a standard below.

---

## BS-1 First impression

1.1 The sign outside is clean and every part of it that lights up is lit.

1.2 The entrance, the windows and the car park or pavement in front are free of litter.

1.3 The opening hours on the door are the hours the site trades.

## BS-2 Welcome and service

2.1 A customer who reaches the counter or the till is greeted within 30 seconds.

2.2 Every colleague in front of customers wears the full uniform and a name badge.

2.3 When more than five customers wait at one open till, another till is opened.

## BS-3 Cleanliness

3.1 Floors, tables and counters are clean and dry. A spill is cleaned and signed at once.

3.2 The customer toilets are checked every hour while the site trades, and the check sheet on the door is signed each time.

3.3 No bin a customer can see is more than three-quarters full.

## BS-4 Product and availability

4.1 Every product on the menu board or the shelf plan is on sale. A product that is out of stock carries an out-of-stock label.

4.2 A product on display looks like its photograph in the product guide.

4.3 No product on display is past the date on its label.

## BS-5 Prices and promotions

5.1 The price on the shelf edge or the menu board is the price at the till.

5.2 Only current promotional material is on show. Material for a promotion that has ended is taken down within 24 hours of its end.

## BS-6 Equipment and maintenance

6.1 A fault in a piece of equipment is written in the maintenance log within the shift in which it is found.

6.2 No equipment a customer uses is out of use for more than 7 days without a log entry and a date for its repair.

## BS-7 Safety

7.1 Every fire exit is clear and unlocked while the site trades.

7.2 A wet floor has a wet floor sign beside it until it is dry.

7.3 The first aid box is sealed and fully stocked.

## BS-8 Records and people

8.1 The training record of every colleague on shift is up to date.

8.2 The daily checks for the day before are signed.

## What this document does not cover

- Food safety limits and checks: see the Food Safety Plan.
- How a job is done step by step: see the Store Procedures Manual.
- What is done about a finding, by whom and by when: the area manager decides and agrees it with the site.
- Any judgement about a colleague. A visit scores the site against the standards. It does not score a person.
