# Franchise Operations Manual (invented)

Rowanmere Group is invented, and so is this document. It is not the manual of any real franchisor.

Version: 3.0. Effective date: 1 September 2026. Owner: Head of Franchise Operations. It covers how a franchised Rowanmere Kitchen restaurant is run from day to day. Amounts are US dollars.

A franchisee also holds a franchise agreement with Rowanmere Group. This manual is not that agreement and does not explain it. Section 7 says where a question about the agreement goes.

## 1. Fees and reporting dates

1.1 The royalty is 6 percent of net sales, and the contribution to the national marketing fund is 3 percent of net sales. Net sales are sales less value added tax.

1.2 The weekly sales report covers Monday to Sunday. It is sent through the franchisee website by 12:00 on the Tuesday after the week ends.

1.3 Rowanmere Group sends one invoice for royalty and marketing fund on the 5th of each month, for the month before. It is payable by the 20th of the month in which it is sent.

1.4 A sales report that is late is chased by the franchise team on the next working day. What follows when a report or a payment stays late is set by the franchise agreement, not by this manual.

## 2. Brand standards audits

2.1 Each franchised restaurant has two audits a year. An audit is not announced. The area manager scores it on the visit checklist against the Brand Standards, version 2.0.

2.2 The checklist has 12 items, each scored 2, 1 or 0, so the highest score is 24. A score of 20 or more is a pass.

2.3 A restaurant that scores below 20 sends an action plan to the area manager within 14 days of the audit, and is audited again within 60 days of the audit.

2.4 A franchisee who thinks a score is wrong may ask for it to be looked at again, in writing to the Head of Franchise Operations, within 7 days of receiving the audit report.

2.5 The Brand Standards apply to a franchised restaurant in full. A question about what a standard requires is answered from the Brand Standards.

## 3. Menu and pricing freedom

3.1 The core menu is sold in full, at every hour the restaurant trades.

3.2 A franchisee may add up to three local items to the menu. Each one needs the menu team's written approval, asked for at least 30 days before the item goes on sale.

3.3 A franchisee sets the restaurant's own prices, up to 10 percent above the recommended price list. During a national promotion, the promoted items are sold at the promotion price.

3.4 A local item is not sold until the menu team has added it to the Allergen Matrix.

## 4. Local marketing

4.1 A franchisee spends at least 1 percent of net sales each year on local marketing, and keeps the receipts for the yearly review.

4.2 Local marketing uses the artwork on the brand portal. Artwork a franchisee has made needs the marketing team's approval before it is used. Approval takes up to 10 working days.

4.3 A local price promotion needs the marketing team's approval, asked for at least 14 days before it starts.

4.4 Each restaurant may have one social media page, named "Rowanmere Kitchen" and the street of the restaurant.

## 5. Equipment and refits

5.1 Ovens, chillers, freezers, fryers and the point of sale are bought from the approved equipment list on the brand portal.

5.2 A restaurant is refitted to the current design every 7 years, counted from its opening or its last refit.

5.3 The franchisee pays for a refit. The property team approves the plans before any work starts.

5.4 A fault in an oven, a chiller or a freezer is reported to an approved engineer on the day it is found.

## 6. Supply through approved suppliers

6.1 All food and all packaging are bought from the approved supplier list.

6.2 A franchisee may propose a local supplier for fresh produce only. The supply team decides within 30 days, after a food safety audit of the supplier.

6.3 When an approved supplier fails to deliver, a franchisee may buy milk, fresh produce and ice from a local shop for up to 3 days, and tells the supply team within 24 hours of the first purchase.

6.4 An order placed by 14:00 is delivered two days later.

## 7. What this manual does not cover

7.1 The franchise agreement itself: its length, renewal, the territory, the sale or transfer of a franchise, ending the agreement, changes to the fee rates and disputes. A question about any of these goes to the franchise team at franchise.team@rowanmere.example. This manual gives no answer to them.

7.2 The pay, hours and contracts of a franchisee's own colleagues. They are the franchisee's own matter.

7.3 Food safety limits and checks: see the Food Safety Plan. How a job is done step by step: see the Store Procedures Manual. Allergens: see the Allergen Matrix.

7.4 A question this manual does not answer goes to the franchise team. A guess is not an answer.
