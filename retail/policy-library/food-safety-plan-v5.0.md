# Rowanmere Kitchen (an invented company, for demonstration only)
# Food Safety Plan

**SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION. Every limit, schedule and name here is invented. It is not a food safety plan for any real operation and is not food safety guidance.**

- Document: Food Safety Plan
- Version: 5.0
- Effective date: 1 July 2026
- Owner: Head of Food Safety and Quality

## Section 1. Scope

1.1 This plan sets the checks every restaurant must make and record, how often, and the limit for each.

1.2 A reading outside a limit, or a check that was not made, is a deviation. Every deviation needs a corrective action recorded by the restaurant manager and reviewed by the food safety lead.

1.3 Whether food was safe after a deviation is decided by the restaurant manager and the food safety lead. No record and no report decides that.

## Section 2. Cold storage

| Ref | Unit | Limit | Check | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Walk-in chiller and reach-in chillers | 1.0 °C to 5.0 °C | Twice a day: at opening, and between 15:00 and 17:00 | Temperature log |
| 2.2 | Freezers | −18.0 °C or colder | Once a day, at opening | Temperature log |

2.3 A chiller reading above 5.0 °C is a deviation. A second reading is taken after 15 minutes. If the second reading is above 8.0 °C the restaurant manager is called before any food from that unit is used.

## Section 3. Cooking and hot holding

| Ref | Step | Limit | Check | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Cooked chicken, core temperature | 75.0 °C or hotter | First batch of each shift | Temperature log |
| 3.2 | Hot holding cabinet | 63.0 °C or hotter | Every two hours while holding food | Temperature log |

3.3 A hot holding reading below 63.0 °C is a deviation. Food that has been below 63.0 °C for more than two hours is discarded; the restaurant manager decides.

## Section 4. Deliveries

| Ref | Item | Limit | Check | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Chilled delivery | 5.0 °C or colder | One item per delivery | Delivery log |
| 4.2 | Frozen delivery | −15.0 °C or colder | One item per delivery | Delivery log |

4.3 An item outside its limit is refused and the refusal is written on the delivery note.

## Section 5. Cleaning

5.1 The daily cleaning schedule has one entry for each day the restaurant trades, signed by the closing manager.

5.2 The weekly deep clean has one entry in each calendar week, signed by the restaurant manager.

5.3 A day with no signed daily entry is a deviation.

## Section 6. Corrective actions

6.1 Every deviation is given a corrective action by the restaurant manager within 24 hours: what was done with the food, what was done with the equipment, and who was told.

6.2 Three or more readings outside the limit on the same unit in one calendar month are reported to the food safety lead, who decides whether the unit is taken out of use until it is serviced. A check that was not made and a day with no signed entry are deviations under 6.1, and are not counted here.

6.3 The area manager reviews each restaurant's records every month and signs the review.

## Section 7. What this plan does not decide

7.1 This plan does not say whether any particular food was safe. That is a judgement for the restaurant manager and the food safety lead.

7.2 This plan does not cover allergens. See the Allergen Matrix.
