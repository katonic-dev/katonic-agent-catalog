# Working Time and Staffing Rules (invented)

Rowanmere Group is invented, and so is this document. It is not the document of any real retailer.

Version: 1.2. Owner: Head of People. A draft roster is checked against these rules before it is published.

## How hours and times are counted

Hours are counted from the start of a shift to its end, and a break is part of the shift. The roster week runs from Monday to Sunday. A young worker is a colleague aged 16 or 17, and the roster gives the age band of each colleague as adult or young worker. A colleague is on shift at a time when the shift has started and has not ended. A figure exactly on a limit keeps the rule.

## Part A. Working time rules of Meridia, as the company states them

Part A states the working time rules of Meridia as the company states them for its managers. Meridia is an invented country, and these are not the rules of any real country.

### WT-1. Maximum shift length

No shift is longer than 10 hours.

### WT-2. Rest between shifts

A colleague has at least 11 hours of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next.

### WT-3. Maximum weekly hours

A colleague is rostered for no more than 48 hours in a roster week.

### WT-4. Break

A shift longer than 6 hours has a break of at least 30 minutes.

### WT-5. Young workers: shift length

No shift of a young worker is longer than 8 hours.

### WT-6. Young workers: weekly hours

A young worker is rostered for no more than 40 hours in a roster week.

### WT-7. Young workers: early and late hours

No shift of a young worker starts before 06:00 or ends after 22:00.

### WT-8. Weekly rest day

A colleague has at least one day with no shift in a roster week.

## Part B. The company's staffing rules

Part B holds the company's own staffing rules. They apply to every store and every restaurant on every day it trades.

### SR-1. A shift manager on every trading hour

A shift manager is on shift at every time between the site's opening time and its closing time.

### SR-2. Two people to open and to close

At least 2 colleagues are on shift at the site's opening time, and at least 2 at its closing time.

### SR-3. A trained first aider on site

A colleague with a current first aid certificate is on shift at every time between the site's opening time and its closing time. The roster marks each first aider.

## What these rules do not decide

These rules do not say who should work which shift, and they do not say how a breach is put right: the manager who owns the roster changes it. They set no limit by a colleague's contracted hours, which are on the roster for the manager's use. They say nothing about pay, and they rate no colleague.
