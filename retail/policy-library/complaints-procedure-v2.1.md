# Complaints Procedure (invented)

Rowanmere Group is invented, and so is this document. It is not the procedure of any real retailer.

Version: 2.1. Owner: Customer Relations Lead. It covers complaints about Rowanmere Market's stores and online shop.

## 1. What a complaint is

1.1 A complaint is any message in which a customer says they are unhappy and expects an answer. Every complaint is logged with its class and the date its response is due.

## 2. The four classes

2.1 Class A, written safety_or_legal. The complaint describes an injury, an illness, an allergic reaction or a hazard, or the customer says they will take legal action. Response due within 2 days of the day the complaint was received.
2.2 Class B, written repeat_contact. The contact history shows two or more earlier contacts about the same matter. Response due within 5 days of the day the complaint was received.
2.3 Class C, written service_failure. We did not do what we said we would: a late, missing, damaged or wrong delivery, or the way a colleague or a driver behaved. Fewer than two earlier contacts. Response due within 10 days of the day the complaint was received.
2.4 Class D, written policy_disagreement. We did what our policy says and the customer disagrees with the policy or the charge. Response due within 14 days of the day the complaint was received.
2.5 When more than one class fits, the complaint takes the first that fits in the order A, B, C, D.
2.6 Days are calendar days. The day the complaint was received is day 0.

## 3. What a response holds

3.1 It thanks the customer and says in one sentence what the complaint is about.
3.2 It says what our records show, with the dates, and says so when the records do not explain what happened.
3.3 It says what happens next and by when.
3.4 It admits no fault and offers no money, credit or goodwill. The Customer Relations Lead decides those.

## 4. Who decides

4.1 A class A complaint goes to the Customer Relations Lead on the day it is received. No finding and no response is sent before the Lead has read it.
4.2 The Customer Relations Lead approves every response before it is sent, and decides any goodwill payment.

## 5. What this procedure does not decide

This procedure does not decide whether the company was at fault, and it does not decide what a customer is paid. It sets the class, the date and who decides.
