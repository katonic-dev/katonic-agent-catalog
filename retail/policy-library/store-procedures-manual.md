## SP-01 Opening checks (version 2.3)

1.1 The opening manager arrives at least 60 minutes before the restaurant opens.

1.2 Before any food is prepared, the opening manager records the temperature of every chiller and freezer in the temperature log (see SP-04).

1.3 The opening manager checks that the handwash basins have hot water, soap and paper towels, and signs the opening checklist.

1.4 Ovens are switched on 45 minutes before opening and the oven temperature is recorded once it is steady.

1.5 If a chiller or freezer is outside its limit at opening, follow SP-04 section 4.5 before using any food from it.

## SP-04 Temperature checks (version 2.0)

4.1 Chillers are checked and recorded twice a day: at opening and between 15:00 and 17:00.

4.2 Freezers are checked and recorded once a day, at opening.

4.3 The hot holding cabinet is checked and recorded every two hours while it holds food.

4.4 The limits are set by the Food Safety Plan, not by this manual. Read them there.

4.5 If a reading is outside the limit: take a second reading with the probe thermometer after 15 minutes, tell the restaurant manager, and record both readings and the action taken. The restaurant manager decides what happens to the food.

4.6 A missed check is recorded as missed, with the reason. Never fill in a reading afterwards from memory.

## SP-07 Order remake (version 1.4)

7.1 An order is remade, at no charge to the guest, when: (a) a topping is wrong or missing, (b) the pizza is undercooked or burnt, (c) the product has stood on the hot rack for more than 15 minutes, or (d) the box is damaged so that the product is affected.

7.2 The crew member who finds the fault tells the shift manager before remaking. The shift manager approves the remake.

7.3 Every remake is recorded on the point of sale with a reason code: R1 wrong item, R2 quality at the oven, R3 hold time exceeded, R4 damaged in handling, R5 guest request.

7.4 A photo of the faulty product is taken on the store tablet before it is discarded, for reason codes R1 and R2.

7.5 More than six remakes in one shift is reported to the area manager in the shift handover.

7.6 This procedure does not cover refunds or compensation. Those are decided by the restaurant manager under the Guest Recovery Policy.

## SP-09 Click and collect handover (version 3.1)

9.1 Before handing over a click and collect order, the crew member asks for the order number.

9.2 For an order paid online, the crew member also sees a photo ID in the name on the order (section 4.2 of the Guest Recovery Policy gives the accepted IDs).

9.3 Someone collecting on the customer's behalf gives the order number and the customer's name, and shows their own photo ID. The crew member writes the collector's name on the order slip.

9.4 Energy drinks are not handed to anyone who appears to be under 16.

9.5 An order not collected within 45 minutes of its ready time is discarded and recorded as uncollected. The guest is offered a remake when they arrive.

## SP-11 Receiving a delivery (version 2.2)

11.1 The receiving crew member checks the delivery against the delivery note before the driver leaves: items, quantities and use-by dates.

11.2 The temperature of one chilled item and one frozen item from each delivery is taken with the probe thermometer and recorded in the delivery log. The limits are in the Food Safety Plan.

11.3 A shortfall, a damaged case or a temperature outside the limit is written on the delivery note and signed by the driver. The item outside its temperature limit is refused.

11.4 Chilled and frozen items are put away within 15 minutes of arrival.

## SP-12 Cash handling (version 1.8)

12.1 Each till is counted at the start and at the end of a shift by the shift manager, with a second crew member present.

12.2 A difference of more than USD 20 on a till is recorded and reported to the restaurant manager the same day.

12.3 Cash above USD 2,000 in a till is moved to the safe.

12.4 Delivery drivers carry a float of no more than USD 150.

## SP-15 Accidents and incidents (version 2.1)

15.1 Any accident to a crew member or a guest is reported to the shift manager at once.

15.2 The shift manager completes the incident form before the end of the shift. A burn, a cut needing more than a plaster, a fall, or any report of a guest becoming ill is also reported to the area manager by phone the same day.

15.3 Do not admit fault or discuss compensation with a guest. Take their contact details and tell them the restaurant manager will call.

## SP-18 Closing checks (version 2.0)

18.1 The cleaning schedule for the day is signed off line by line by the closing manager.

18.2 All food in the hot holding cabinet is discarded at close.

18.3 The closing manager records that ovens are off, the back door is locked and the alarm is set.

## What this manual does not cover

- A customer who has no ID at click and collect: ask the restaurant manager.
- Any question about pay, leave or rosters: see the Staff Handbook.
- Allergen questions: see the Allergen Matrix. Never answer an allergen question from this manual.
