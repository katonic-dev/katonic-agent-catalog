# Rowanmere Group (an invented company, for demonstration only)
# Staff Handbook

**SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION. Every policy, number and name here is invented. It is not the policy of any real employer and is not legal or HR advice.**

- Document: Staff Handbook
- Version: 2.4
- Effective date: 1 January 2026
- Owner: Head of People

## HR-01 Annual leave

1.1 Full-time colleagues have 21 working days of paid annual leave in each of their first five years, and 30 working days from the sixth year.

1.2 Leave is requested in the people system at least 14 days before the first day of leave. Leave of more than 10 working days in a row is requested at least 30 days before.

1.3 Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year and must be taken by 31 March.

1.4 Leave during the two busiest trading weeks of the year is approved by the area manager for restaurant colleagues, because of trading levels.

## HR-02 Sick leave

2.1 A colleague who is ill tells their manager before the start of the shift, by phone.

2.2 A medical certificate is needed for an absence of more than two days in a row.

2.3 A colleague with vomiting or diarrhoea does not work with food and returns only 48 hours after the symptoms stop. Tell the restaurant manager; this is a food safety rule.

## HR-03 Working hours and shift swaps

3.1 Rosters are published by Thursday for the week starting the following Sunday.

3.2 Two colleagues may swap a shift if both hold the same station training and the shift manager approves the swap in the people system at least 24 hours before the shift.

3.3 Nobody works more than six days in a row.

## HR-04 Expenses

4.1 Business expenses are claimed in the finance system within 30 days of the date on the receipt, with the receipt attached.

4.2 Travel between restaurants in a colleague's own car is paid at USD 0.90 per kilometre.

4.3 A single expense above USD 1,000 needs the line manager's approval before it is spent.

4.4 Meals while travelling are paid up to USD 120 a day.

## HR-05 Uniform

5.1 Restaurant colleagues receive three shirts, two aprons, one cap and one name badge on joining, at no charge.

5.2 A replacement for a worn item is free after six months. A lost item is replaced at cost.

5.3 Closed, non-slip shoes are worn in the kitchen. They are bought by the colleague; the company pays up to USD 150 once a year against a receipt.

## HR-06 Training

6.1 Every new restaurant colleague completes food safety level 1 and the allergen briefing before working a shift without a trainer.

6.2 Station training is signed off by the shift manager in the training record.

6.3 Shift managers complete food safety level 2 within three months of promotion.

## HR-07 Raising a concern

7.1 A concern about safety, conduct or pay is raised first with the line manager, or with the People team if it concerns the line manager.

7.2 A concern may be raised in confidence through the speak-up line. The handbook does not publish the number; it is on the notice board in every restaurant and office.

## What this handbook does not cover

- An individual's pay, contract or visa status: ask the People team.
- Payments due when a colleague leaves the company: these follow the contract; ask the People team.
- Disciplinary outcomes: these are decided by a manager and the People team under the disciplinary procedure, which is a separate document.
