# Campaign Brief Template (invented)

Rowanmere Group is invented, and so is this document. It is not the document of any real retailer.

Version: 1.0. Owner: Head of Marketing. Every campaign of the Rowanmere Market loyalty programme starts with a brief in these eleven sections, in this order.

## 1. Title

The name of the campaign, in six words or fewer.

## 2. Objective

One sentence, with a number and a date: what the campaign is to achieve and by when.

## 3. Audience

Who it is for, how many members that is, and the consent it rests on. The size comes from the programme's totals and is never larger than the number of members who have opted in to the channel.

## 4. Offer

What the member gets, on which lines, and for how long.

## 5. Channels

Email, the app, in store or the website. A member is contacted only through a channel they have opted in to.

## 6. Timing

The first and the last day.

## 7. Budget

The whole cost in US dollars.

## 8. Measures of success

What will be counted afterwards, each with its target.

## 9. What last year showed

The past campaign most like this one, by its id, with its figures as the records give them: members sent to, redemptions, sales, cost and return for each dollar. A past campaign that returned less than a dollar for each dollar is said to have done so.

## 10. Rules and risks

Consent, age-restricted lines and anything else the marketing lead should weigh. An offer that includes an age-restricted line goes to adults only.

## 11. Open points for the marketing lead

What the brief could not settle from the records.

## What a brief does not do

A brief sends nothing. The marketing lead owns the brief and approves the campaign before anything is sent to a member.
