# Brand Voice Guide (invented)

Rowanmere Group is invented, and so is this document. It is not the guide of any real retailer.

Version: 1.2. Owner: Head of Customer Care. It covers every reply Rowanmere Market posts under a public review, on any review site.

## 1. How a reply sounds

V-1 Open by thanking the reviewer, using the display name they chose. Use no other name.
V-2 Write in plain words and short sentences. A reply is no longer than 80 words.
V-3 Write as "we". Sound like a helpful colleague in the store, not like a notice.
V-4 Use no exclamation marks, no capitals for emphasis and no emoji.

## 2. Praise

V-5 Say thank you once, name what the reviewer liked, and say we will pass it to the team at that store. Add no offer and no advertisement.

## 3. Criticism

V-6 Apologise once, for the thing the reviewer describes. Give no excuse and no explanation of how the store works.
V-7 Never argue, and never say the reviewer is mistaken, even when our records differ.
V-8 Offer no money, voucher, refund or replacement in public. The care team decides those in private.
V-9 Invite the reviewer to write to care@rowanmere-market.example with their order number, so that a member of the care team can look into it. Ask for no personal details in the reply itself.

## 4. A review with praise and criticism

V-10 Thank the reviewer for the praise first, then follow section 3 for the criticism. One reply, still no longer than 80 words.

## 5. Reviews we do not reply to before a person has read them

V-11 A review that describes an injury, an illness, an allergic reaction, something found in food, a hazard in a store, or that mentions a lawyer, a claim or legal action, gets no drafted reply. It is marked for the Customer Relations Lead, who reads it first and decides what is said.
V-12 A review that names a colleague, or accuses a colleague of something, is replied to without the colleague's name.

## 6. How a reply ends

V-13 End every reply with the line: The Rowanmere Market team
