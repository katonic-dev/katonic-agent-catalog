# Home Buildings and Contents Policy Wording

Thornmere Mutual Insurance (invented). Wording TMI-HOM-02, version 2.

> Thornmere Mutual Insurance is invented, and so is this policy wording. It is written for demonstration and is not a contract of insurance. Nothing here describes a real insurer or a real policy.

## How to read this wording

Section 1 says what is covered. Section 2 says what is not covered. Section 3 gives the most that is paid. Section 4 gives the deductible. Section 5 gives the conditions. Amounts in the policy schedule apply where a clause says so. The wording covers nothing it does not name.

## Section 1. What is covered

**1.1 Fire.** We pay for loss or damage caused by fire, lightning or explosion.

**1.2 Storm or flood.** We pay for loss or damage caused by storm or flood.

**1.3 Escape of water.** We pay for loss or damage caused by the sudden and accidental escape of water from a fixed water pipe, tank or appliance.

**1.4 Theft.** We pay for loss or damage caused by theft or attempted theft following forcible and violent entry to or exit from the home.

**1.5 Alternative accommodation.** When the home cannot be lived in after loss or damage we cover, we pay the reasonable cost of somewhere else to live.

**1.6 Trace and access.** We pay the cost of finding the source of an escape of water we cover, and of making good the damage done in finding it.

## Section 2. What is not covered

**2.1 Gradual damage.** Water that escapes gradually, seepage, rising damp, and any damage that happened over a period of time.

**2.2 Unoccupied home.** Loss or damage that happens after the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 days in a row.

**2.3 Theft without forced entry.** Theft where there was no forcible and violent entry to or exit from the home.

**2.4 Wear and tear.** Wear and tear, and damage caused by pets or vermin.

**2.5 Improvements.** The cost of improving the home beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

## Section 3. The most we pay

**3.1 Buildings and contents.** The most we pay is the sum insured for buildings and the sum insured for contents in the schedule.

**3.2 Single items.** The most we pay for any one item of contents is USD 2,500, unless the item is listed in the schedule.

**3.3 Alternative accommodation.** The most we pay under clause 1.5 is 10 percent of the sum insured for buildings.

**3.4 Trace and access.** The most we pay under clause 1.6 is USD 1,500 for any one claim.

## Section 4. The deductible

**4.1 The deductible.** The deductible in the schedule is taken once from each claim.

**4.2 Escape of water.** For a claim under clause 1.3 the deductible is USD 500, in place of the deductible in the schedule.

## Section 5. Conditions

**5.1 Telling us.** You must tell us about a loss within 30 days of the date it happened.

**5.2 The police.** Theft or attempted theft must be reported to the police. You must send us the police report.

**5.3 Damaged items.** Keep damaged items until we have had the chance to see them.
