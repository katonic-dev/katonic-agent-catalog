# Private Motor Policy Wording

Thornmere Mutual Insurance (invented). Wording TMI-MOT-01, version 3.

> Thornmere Mutual Insurance is invented, and so is this policy wording. It is written for demonstration and is not a contract of insurance. Nothing here describes a real insurer or a real policy.

## How to read this wording

Section 1 says what is covered. Section 2 says what is not covered. Section 3 gives the most that is paid. Section 4 gives the deductible. Section 5 gives the conditions. Amounts in the policy schedule apply where a clause says so. The wording covers nothing it does not name.

## Section 1. What is covered

**1.1 Loss or damage.** We pay for accidental loss of or damage to the insured vehicle.

**1.2 Theft.** We pay for loss of or damage to the insured vehicle by theft or attempted theft.

**1.3 Damage to other people's property.** We pay what you are legally liable to pay for damage to another person's property caused by an accident involving the insured vehicle, up to the third party property limit in the schedule.

**1.4 Windscreen and glass.** We pay to repair or replace broken glass in the windscreen or windows of the insured vehicle.

**1.5 Hire car.** While the insured vehicle is being repaired after loss or damage we cover, or after its theft, we pay for a hire car.

## Section 2. What is not covered

**2.1 No valid licence.** Loss or damage while the insured vehicle is driven by a person who does not hold a valid driving licence.

**2.2 Wear and tear.** Wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, and damage to tyres by braking, punctures, cuts or bursts.

**2.3 Hire or reward.** Loss or damage while the insured vehicle is used for hire or reward, or for racing or pace-making.

**2.4 Alcohol or drugs.** Loss or damage while the driver is over the legal limit for alcohol or is under the influence of drugs.

**2.5 Cleaning and servicing.** The cost of cleaning, valeting and routine servicing.

**2.6 Loss of value.** Any loss of value of the insured vehicle after it has been repaired.

## Section 3. The most we pay

**3.1 The vehicle.** The most we pay for the insured vehicle is its market value at the date of the loss, and never more than the sum insured in the schedule.

**3.2 Windscreen and glass.** The most we pay under clause 1.4 is USD 500 for any one claim.

**3.3 Personal belongings.** The most we pay for personal belongings in the insured vehicle is USD 300 for any one claim.

**3.4 Hire car.** The most we pay under clause 1.5 is USD 40 a day, for up to 10 days, for any one claim.

## Section 4. The deductible

**4.1 The deductible.** The deductible in the schedule is taken once from each claim.

**4.2 Young driver.** A further USD 300 is taken when the driver at the time of the loss is under 25 years of age.

## Section 5. Conditions

**5.1 Telling us.** You must tell us about a loss within 30 days of the date it happened.

**5.2 The police.** Theft, attempted theft, and any accident in which a person is injured must be reported to the police. You must send us the police report.
